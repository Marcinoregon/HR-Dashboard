📖 Flight Risk & Turnover Methodology

This module analyzes employee retention and separation patterns at Mr. Macky's Cajun Cuisine. Flight risk and turnover metrics are calculated using two primary mathematical models:

📊 Historical Turnover Rate (Overall & Heatmap) Calculates the percentage of separations (quits + discharges) relative to the total historical workforce size (active + separated).
Turnover Rate = (Quits + Discharges) / (Employed + Quits + Discharges) × 100
Heatmap Colors: 🟢 Low (≤ 50%) | 🟡 Medium (> 50%) | 🔴 High (> 70%)
🌡️ Predictive Flight Risk (Engagement-Based) Tracks the count of active employees flagged as potential flight risks due to showing a year-over-year declining trend in survey scores.
Risk Impact = +min(Flagged Employees × 6, 15) points
Contributes directly to the predictive turnover risk score in the Turnover Prediction tab.
